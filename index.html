Ibrahim VPS Backup

A personal backup integration that securely stores encrypted copies of the owner's VPS data in the owner's Google Drive account.

What it does

The application uses rclone to connect to Google Drive and restic to encrypt backup contents before upload. It is intended solely for the owner's disaster recovery and is not offered as a public service.

Only Google Drive files created or explicitly made available to this application are needed. It does not access Gmail, contacts, or unrelated Google account data.

Read our Privacy Policy for details on permissions, security, retention, and revoking access.
